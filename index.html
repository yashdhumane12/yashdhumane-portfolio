About Me.

◆ DevOps Engineer & SRE Pune, IN · Bare-metal infra specialist

I'm a DevOps Engineer at Honcho Minds, working across production and staging environments for several products at once — an AI-powered ITSM platform, a legal-AI product, and a source-code protection service among them.

My infrastructure is entirely self-hosted: a Proxmox hypervisor running a kubeadm-built Kubernetes cluster, self-hosted GitLab CE and Jenkins for CI/CD, and Nginx/HAProxy handling routing and TLS by hand. No managed Kubernetes, no managed CI — when something breaks, there's no support ticket to file, only logs to read.

That constraint has made debugging the core of the job: tracing WebSocket 403s to token-validation order, chasing down nginx DNS caching, rebuilding a Jenkins pipeline around Nuitka-compiled source protection. I like the kind of problem that only shows up once real traffic hits it.

DevOps Engineer with hands-on experience in Docker, Jenkins, Linux server administration, AWS, and Nginx/IIS-based CI/CD pipeline management. Skilled in deployment automation, monitoring, and cross-team collaboration to improve deployment efficiency and infrastructure reliability. Open to DevOps, SRE, and Platform Engineering roles where reliability, security, and automation are non-negotiable.
